Why this screen looks like this

  • Camera first. No menu, no empty form — the scanner is the screen.
  • Two fields. Price, photos, and costs come later from whoever has them.
  • Nothing blocks the save. Completeness is a meter that invites finishing, not validation that refuses.
  • Actions in the bottom third. That is where a thumb reaches.
  • 16px inputs, 44px targets. Below 16px iOS zooms on focus; below 44px a gloved hand misses.
  • Manual entry always one tap away. Windshield barcodes are scratched, dirty, and sometimes absent.

Note the progress meter is neutral grey, not blue. It is not something you can click.
